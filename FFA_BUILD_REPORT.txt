## Permissions / Why Needed
- `BIND_NOTIFICATION_LISTENER_SERVICE` (service binding): required for notification callbacks.
- `BIND_QUICK_SETTINGS_TILE` (service binding): required for Quick Settings tile.
- `android.permission.VIBRATE`: optional hard-coded 40ms vibration on flash trigger.
- `android.permission.QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES` (declared, tools:ignore): used for complete launcher-visible installed-app list for per-app selection as requested; no app data collected/transmitted.
- No CAMERA runtime permission requested; torch uses Camera2 `CameraManager.setTorchMode` / `turnOnTorchWithStrengthLevel`.

## Supported / Unsupported Hardware Capabilities
- Supported when available: rear/available camera flash torch ON/OFF via Camera2; torch strength control on Android 13+ devices exposing `FLASH_INFO_STRENGTH_MAXIMUM_LEVEL > 1`.
- Unsupported/limited: no flash unit (UI reports unavailable); no torch strength support (Dim unavailable, standard torch used); camera-in-use conflicts can block torch; OEM background restrictions may affect delivery.

## Implementation Notes / Known Limitations
- Notification detection: `NotificationListenerService.onNotificationPosted`; no polling, no AccessibilityService.
- Default pattern: initial OFF 120ms, ON 80ms, OFF 120ms, ON 80ms, final OFF (2 pulses). Initial OFF and 2-pulse count are hard-coded. ON/OFF durations configurable via presets/manual inputs.
- Flash while locked/unlocked/silent: hard-coded always enabled; engine does not gate on lock/sound state.
- Vibration: hard-coded 40ms, toggle only in Settings.
- Call detection: via `Notification.CATEGORY_CALL` in NotificationListenerService (permission-conscious; no READ_PHONE_STATE/READ_CALL_LOG). Behavior depends on dialer/OS posting call-category notifications.
- Dedup: StatusBarNotification key/postTime heuristic; grouped notification updates can vary by app/OS.
- No backend/auth/ads/analytics/accessibility/overlay/storage/location/contacts/mic permissions.
